Explanation

Shilajit is a natural resin used for centuries in traditional medicine. It's prized for its rich mineral content—especially fulvic acid and a variety of electrolytes such as potassium, magnesium, sodium and calcium. While many people take Shilajit to boost energy, cognition and overall well-being, some report episodes of heart racing or palpitations. Understanding how Shilajit and heart palpitations may be linked starts with the role of electrolytes in your body and how rapid shifts—or "electrolyte spikes"—can affect heart rhythm.

Key electrolytes for heart rhythm include:

Potassium (K⁺): Regulates the electrical signals that control heartbeat

Sodium (Na⁺): Works with potassium to generate nerve impulses

Magnesium (Mg²⁺): Helps muscles relax and prevents excessive calcium influx

Calcium (Ca²⁺): Triggers muscle contraction

A delicate balance of these charged particles keeps your heartbeat steady. Even small, rapid changes—or spikes—in electrolyte levels can temporarily disrupt cardiac electrical signaling, leading to sensations of fluttering, pounding or racing.

Who Is Most at Risk?

Not everyone who takes Shilajit will experience heart palpitations. However, certain factors can heighten sensitivity:

Pre-existing heart conditions (arrhythmias, atrial fibrillation)

Kidney disease (impaired ability to regulate electrolytes)

Use of diuretics or blood pressure medications (which alter fluid/electrolyte balance)

Dehydration (makes you more susceptible to rapid electrolyte changes)

High-dose supplementation (exceeding manufacturer's recommended amount)

If any of these apply to you, it's wise to monitor how you feel after taking Shilajit and consider checking your electrolyte levels periodically.

Signs and Symptoms of an Electrolyte Spike

Persistent or worsening palpitations

Dizziness, lightheadedness or fainting spells

Shortness of breath

Chest discomfort or tightness

Muscle cramps or weakness

Excessive thirst or dehydration
