# When to Start Prostate Screening? The Critical Age to Act Now | Ubie Doctor's Note

Published on: 7/15/2026

At what age should men start screening for prostate cancer?

Reviewed by Yoshinori Abe, MD

Internal Medicine

Most men should begin discussing prostate cancer screening with their doctor at age 50. However, men at higher risk—including Black men and those with a family history of prostate cancer—should start these conversations earlier, typically between ages 40 and 45.

Key screening guidelines by risk level:

Average risk: Begin discussions at age 50

High risk (family history): Begin at age 45

Highest risk (Black men or multiple affected relatives): Begin at age 40

Screening typically involves a PSA blood test and, in some cases, a digital rectal exam. Early detection significantly improves treatment outcomes, as prostate cancer is highly treatable when caught early.

Risk Factors
