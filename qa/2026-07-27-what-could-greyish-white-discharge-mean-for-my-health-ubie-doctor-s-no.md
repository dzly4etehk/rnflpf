Vaginal discharge is a normal part of the body’s way to keep the vagina clean and moist. A greyish white color can be a normal finding for some people and may vary throughout the menstrual cycle. However, if this discharge comes with a strong, unusual odor (often described as a fishy smell), itching, burning, or irritation, it might be a sign of an infection called bacterial vaginosis. Bacterial vaginosis happens when the normal balance of bacteria in the vagina shifts. Other infections, like trichomoniasis, may also cause changes in color or consistency; although trichomoniasis often causes yellow-green or frothy discharge. Any change that worries you should be checked out by a doctor. It is important to remember that every person is different. If your greyish white discharge has always been the same and does not bother you, it may be a normal variation. But if you notice any new symptoms or discomfort, it is a good idea to see a healthcare provider who can do tests and offer the right treatment if needed.
