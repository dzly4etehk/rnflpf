Explanation

When your lips feel sore but aren’t dry or cracked like chapped lips, it might be because of a type of lip inflammation. One cause is sun damage, which over time may lead to a condition called actinic cheilitis. This happens with too much sun on your lips and can make them hurt. Other reasons include irritants—like certain foods, cosmetics, or even touching your lips too much—that can cause a reaction without making your lips visibly dry. Sometimes, an allergic response might make your lips sore even though they don’t look chapped. In some cases, an infection or an ongoing inflammation process can cause discomfort too. There are many reasons to consider, and it might help to notice if other symptoms, like swelling or color changes, are showing up along with pain. It is a good idea to see a doctor or dentist if this soreness continues so they can look at all the details and tell you exactly why your lips hurt.
