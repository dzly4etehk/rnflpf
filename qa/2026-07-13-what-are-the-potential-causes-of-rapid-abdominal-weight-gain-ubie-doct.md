Explanation

Rapid abdominal weight gain can happen for several reasons. One cause can be rapid weight gain after birth, which is linked to more fat around the belly. This is important because having more fat in this area can lead to health issues later on.

Another reason might be a condition called idiopathic gastroparesis. This condition affects how the stomach empties food, and can lead to changes in body weight. People with this condition might experience weight gain due to the way their body processes food.
