Published on: 7/14/2026

What might be causing a weird feeling in my lower right abdomen?

Reviewed by Yoshinori Abe, MD

Internal Medicine

Pain or discomfort in the lower right side of your abdomen can stem from many causes. Common culprits include muscle strain, digestive inflammation (such as appendicitis, Crohn's disease, or diverticulitis), ovarian cysts, kidney stones, or a hernia. Less common causes include iliopsoas bursitis, which may be linked to sleeping posture or repetitive hip movement.

Because symptoms often overlap between minor and serious conditions, self-diagnosis can be risky. Some causes—like appendicitis or an ectopic pregnancy—require urgent care, while others resolve with rest. Understanding the likely source of your pain is the first step toward the right treatment.
