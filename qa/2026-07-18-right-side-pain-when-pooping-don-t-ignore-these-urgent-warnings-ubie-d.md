Explanation

Pain on the right side during bowel movements can have several causes. One possible reason is faecal retention, where stool builds up in the intestine, causing discomfort and pain. This condition can lead to functional bowel issues, which means the bowel doesn't work properly even though there's no clear disease causing it.

Another potential cause is lumbosacral radicular pain, which is pain that comes from nerves in the lower back area. When these nerves are irritated or compressed, it can cause pain during activities like pooping or peeing. This type of pain might feel sharp or like a shooting pain down the leg.

Understanding these causes can help in determining the right approach to managing the pain.
