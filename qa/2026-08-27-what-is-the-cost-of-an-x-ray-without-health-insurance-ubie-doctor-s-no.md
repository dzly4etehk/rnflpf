# What is the cost of an X-ray without health insurance? | Ubie Doctor's Note

What is the cost of an X-ray without health insurance?

Reviewed by Yoshinori Abe, MD

Internal Medicine

An X-ray without insurance typically costs between $100 and $1,000, depending on the body part scanned, facility type, and location. Standalone imaging centers usually offer lower prices than hospitals, while emergency rooms tend to charge the most. Costs may also vary based on whether a radiologist's interpretation fee is included. Like CT scans and other imaging procedures, prices can differ widely, so it's worth comparing providers or asking about self-pay discounts before scheduling.
