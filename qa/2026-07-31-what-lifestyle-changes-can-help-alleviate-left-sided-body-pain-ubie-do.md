Internal Medicine

Changing your daily habits—like being more physically active, eating right, and managing stress—can help reduce pain on one side of your body.

Important Next Steps

Not seeing your question? No worries.

Explanation

One key strategy is to add gentle exercise or physical activity into your day. Regular movement, such as walking, stretching, or light workouts, helps keep your muscles strong and your body flexible. When you move your body, your circulation improves, and this may ease pain that lingers on one side. Eating a healthy, balanced diet can also make a difference. Foods high in minerals, vitamins and anti-oxidants may help reduce discomfort. Watching your diet helps you keep your weight in a healthy range too, which takes pressure off your joints and muscles. Lastly, managing stress really matters. When you learn ways to relax, like deep breathing, quiet time, or gentle yoga, it helps lower body tension that would have contributed to your pain. These changes do not instantly remove pain, but over time, they can help you feel better and improve your overall wellness. If your pain persists or worsens, please talk to your doctor for a complete work up and treatment options.
