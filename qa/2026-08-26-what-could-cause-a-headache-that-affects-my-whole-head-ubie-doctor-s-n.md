Headaches that feel like they affect your entire head can be caused by a variety of health conditions. One common cause is known as tension-type headaches, which are often described as a constant pressure or tightness around the head. These headaches are influenced by muscle tension in the neck, face, scalp, and jaw.

Factors Affecting Mental Health: Stress, intense work, anxiety, and depression are some triggers of tension-type headaches.

Sleep-related Factors: Not getting enough sleep and/or sleep apnea are examples of sleep-related factors that can trigger tension-type headaches.

Understanding the cause of your headaches is a key step toward feeling better. If you find that your headaches are frequent, severe, persistent, worsening, and/or accompanied by other symptoms (such as vomiting, blurry vision, and/or weakness), it is important to talk to a healthcare provider for personalized medical advice.
