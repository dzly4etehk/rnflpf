What might cause tightness in the neck, and how can it be treated?

Reviewed by Yoshinori Abe, MD

Internal Medicine

Neck tightness is most often caused by muscle strain, poor posture, stress, or prolonged screen time. It typically feels like stiffness, reduced range of motion, or a pulling sensation in the neck and upper shoulders. Common treatments include manual therapy, targeted stretching exercises, heat application, and posture correction.

Explanation

Neck tightness often happens because the muscles in the neck, like the trapezius, get tight or don't get enough blood. This can make your neck feel stiff and uncomfortable. If you're experiencing persistent neck discomfort, try Ubie's free AI symptom checker to better understand your symptoms and explore potential next steps.

Treatments:

Manual Therapy: This involves a therapist using their hands to help relax and move the muscles and joints in your neck. It can help reduce tightness and improve how your neck feels.

Stretching Exercises: Doing special exercises that stretch your neck and shoulder muscles can also help. These exercises can make your muscles more flexible and less tight.
