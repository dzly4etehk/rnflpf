Period cramps, also known as menstrual cramps, are a common discomfort that many people experience during their menstrual cycle. These cramps usually feel like a dull or throbbing pain in the lower belly. Sometimes, it can feel like muscle spasms or a heavy, aching sensation.

Key Points:

Location: The pain is typically felt in the lower abdomen and may spread to the lower back or thighs.

Sensation: It can vary from mild to intense and may feel like a squeezing or tightening sensation.

Timing: Cramps often start a day or two before the period begins and can last for a few days.

Research has shown that physical activity can help reduce the intensity of menstrual pain. Engaging in regular exercise may help lessen cramps by improving blood flow and releasing endorphins, which are natural pain relievers. Additionally, abdominal muscle activity has been noted to precede the onset of cramps, suggesting that muscle contractions play a role in the sensation of pain experienced during menstruation.
