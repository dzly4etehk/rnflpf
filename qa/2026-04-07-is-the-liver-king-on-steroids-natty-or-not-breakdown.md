The Liver King may be able to walk to Europe, but I’m not sure he can swim. After all, we’re not fish.

Many cultures still eat raw meat, consume fermented foods, and live in environments that resemble ancestral ways, far removed from the modern world. But is this lifestyle optimal for humans today? Probably not.
