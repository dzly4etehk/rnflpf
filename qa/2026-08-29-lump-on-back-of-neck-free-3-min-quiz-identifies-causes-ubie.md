About the Symptom

A lump on the back of the neck can result from enlarged lymph nodes, cysts, abscesses, or tumors. It may be tender or painless and can vary in size and consistency.

When to See a Doctor

Seek professional care if you experience any of the following symptoms

Rapidly enlarging lump

Lump that doesn't move

Lump persists over 2 weeks

Associated weight loss or fever

Skin changes over lump

Difficulty swallowing or breathing

Night sweats

Recent infections
