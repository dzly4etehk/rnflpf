Eye twitching, medically known as myokymia, is typically harmless and often triggered by everyday factors like stress, fatigue, eye strain, excessive caffeine, or dry eyes. In most cases, it resolves on its own with rest and lifestyle adjustments. However, persistent or severe twitching can occasionally signal underlying health conditions such as nerve disorders, magnesium deficiency, or dry eye syndrome that may require medical attention.

Explanation

Eye twitching, or myokymia, is the involuntary, repetitive spasm of the eyelid muscles. Common causes include:

Stress and fatigue: Lack of sleep or high stress levels often trigger twitching. Eye strain: Long hours looking at screens or needing vision correction can cause eye muscle fatigue. Caffeine or alcohol: Excess intake may lead to muscle spasms. Dry eyes: Insufficient tear production or irritation can trigger twitching. Nutritional imbalances: Deficiencies in magnesium or other nutrients may contribute. Allergies: Eye irritation from allergies can cause twitching.
