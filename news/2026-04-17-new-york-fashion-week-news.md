# New York Fashion Week News

[York Fashion](https://tendonforge.dns0663.com/)

Some of the coolest stuff happening during New York Fashion Week isn’t on the runways. Here’s a quick roundup of the most exciting launches and collaborations that added to the buzz.

Pop-up pantsDockers celebrated the launch of the Alpha Collection last week, but the party continues for a month with it’s first pop up shop at 25 Howard Street in downtown Manhattan. The short-term shop has plenty to hold your attention, including a custom khaki bar, where customers can execute their own vision using 10 colors and two fits shipped directly to their house. Beard need trimming? Every Tuesday through Thursday, The Blind Barber will provide quick clean ups for shoppers. Other freebies and special promotions abound, so if you’re in the city, stop by.

Head east, young manShopBop, the popular online shopping destination bookmarked on your girlfriend’s computer just launched their men’s only shop, East Dane. The site offers your favorite go-to brands, like Rag & Bone, J Brand, Levi’s Made & Crafted, Billykirk and Jack Spade, among dozens of others, with free worldwide shipping and returns, and 24-7 customer service, so you can find out how those A.P.C. New Standard jeans fit (answer: skinny). Curated shops as well as shared shopping cart with your ShopBop loving special lady makes making decisions even easier. It’s definitely worth a look-see.

Chic shadesMercedes-Benz and Shwood Eyewear joined forces this week to celebrate New York’s Mercedes Benz Fashion Week and the new S-Class, a ride known for its advanced sensory elements. The new luxury sedan includes twin-turbo V8 engine and a 7-speed automatic transmission. Can’t afford those wheels on your humble paycheck? Then settle for the sleek, customized Shwood sunglasses made from the eight dashboard woods available in the new car model. Luxury is never cheap: each are available for $595 each exclusively at shwoodshop.com.
