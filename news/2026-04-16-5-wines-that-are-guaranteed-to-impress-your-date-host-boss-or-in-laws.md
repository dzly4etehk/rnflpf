Taste in wine is obviously subjective: What you love, your drinking companions may hate. Even among professional wine judges, ratings are inconsistent, research shows.

So how do you pick a wine that’s sure to impress? Find a bottle with a great backstory.

Each of the wines on this list is not only excellent, but it also has conversation-starting appeal. Even if your boss or host doesn’t love what’s inside, he’ll remember you brought a bottle with some personality.

THE DATE NIGHT WHITE

Mas Julien Blanc 2011 Languedoc, France

Long before it was trendy, winemaker Oliver Julien focused on organic, sustainable, small-production farming techniques to make his wine, says Andrea Morris, a master sommelier and beverage director at New York City’s Nix restaurant.

Now other winemakers are following Julien’s lead, and he’s attained “cult status” for his forward-looking approach to winemaking, she says. Your date will devour these details—and the delicious vino.

($35, wine-searcher.com)

THE HOLIDAY PARTY PICK

Montés Alpha Carmenére 2012 Colchagua Valley, Chile

Your host may stick the bottle on a shelf with a half-dozen others, so you don’t want to spend too much.

For a high-value red with some cool creds, Carmenére is “a crazy wine for Cabernet lovers,” says Madeline Puckette, founder of the wine blog Wine Folly.

[Guaranteed to Impress](https://mendstride.snaketongueofficial.com/blog/5322353976.html)

“The Chileans were planting this grape all over Chile, thinking it was Merlot,” she says. Turns out, they were actually planting a super-rare Bordeaux grape called Carmenére, which is now almost extinct in France.

“People either seem to love or hate Carmenére,” Puckette says. Throw that out there from the start—along with this wine’s 91-point rating—and your host won’t blame you if he’s not a fan.
