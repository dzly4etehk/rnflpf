# The ‘Across the Spider-Verse’ Sequel Is Definitely Happening

When Spider-Man: Into the Spider-Verse came out in 2018, it felt like a reinvention of the superhero story. Stunning animation, strong voice acting, and a stellar storyline made the movie one of the biggest Marvel releases outside of the main MCU franchise. In November 2018, it was no surprise when Variety announced the film would have both a sequel and a spin-off. While the television spin-off is still in development, the film's sequel has already been released.

Spider-Man: Across the Spider-Verse is now available to stream on Netflix. Overall reviews are positive. Brian Davids from The Hollywood Reporter tweeted the film was "darker and sadder than I expected, but necessary." And Rotten Tomatoes writer Tessa Smith tweeted the film "raises its own bar which seemed impossible."

For anyone who needs a refresher, the film takes place a year after the events of Into the Spider-Verse. Miles Morales (Shameik Moore) once again needs to traverse the multiverse to help Gwen Stacy (Hailee Steinfeld) defeat supervillain the Spot (played by Jason Schwartzman). They also encounter the head of the Spider Society, led by Miguel O'Hara (Oscar Isaac).

Now two movies into this new adaptation of Spider-Man, viewers are probably wondering what's next for Miles. Will there be another Spider-Verse film? Or is the television spin-off the last we'll see of the characters we've grown to love on screen? Here's what we know.

Will there be a sequel to Spider-Man: Across the Spider-Verse?

Yes. According to the writer-producers, a third film is already in the works and it's called Spider-Man: Beyond the Spider-Verse. Speaking to Entertainment Weekly, writer-producers Phil Lord and Chris Miller said back in 2021 they had too many ideas for just one movie. So Across the Spider-Verse is part one in an "epic."
