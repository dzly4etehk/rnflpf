Understanding the Etiology of Vertical Lip Lines

Vertical lip lines are primarily caused by intrinsic aging and environmental stressors. Collagen loss and repetitive lip movements over time lead to fine lines forming above and around the mouth. Sun exposure, smoking, and genetics also play a significant role in how and when these lines emerge.

These perioral lines appear in both smokers and non-smokers, particularly in individuals over 40. Apart from aesthetics, they also affect function, causing lipstick to bleed, making the upper lip appear rigid, and creating a tired expression. Early intervention with soft fillers can address both appearance and structure.

Injection Protocols and Clinical Techniques

Precision is essential when addressing vertical lip lines with Juvéderm fillers. The following protocols and techniques offer guidance for optimal placement, product behavior, and natural results:

Microdroplet injections: Ideal for fine perioral lines, microdroplets deliver small volumes of filler into the superficial dermis to minimize the risk of lumpiness and maximize smooth blending.

Superficial threading: This technique involves gently threading filler just beneath the skin’s surface to soften etched-in lines and improve skin texture.

Serial puncture technique: Involves placing multiple tiny injections directly into or adjacent to each line, enabling controlled filler distribution and precise targeting.

Linear threading: A thin thread of filler is deposited as the needle or cannula is withdrawn along the length of the wrinkle. It’s useful for shallow to moderately deep lines.

Fanning technique: Multiple linear threads are placed from a single-entry point in a fan-shaped pattern. This helps treat broader zones efficiently.

Needle vs. cannula: Needles offer more control and precision in the perioral area, especially for superficial work. However, cannulas can be safer for minimizing vascular risks in select cases.

Dosage control: Conservative amounts, typically 0.1 to 0.3 ml per side, are sufficient.

Combining Therapies for Enhanced Results

Combinatory approaches yield superior results in complex perioral aging. Neurotoxins such as Botox can be used to reduce muscle overactivity, allowing filler to settle and maintain shape longer.

Skin boosters, polynucleotides, or biostimulators like Skinvive by Juvéderm can enhance dermal hydration and elasticity, improving filler integration and long-term skin health. Layering therapies over sessions ensures natural-looking rejuvenation.

Patient Education and Consent

Pre-treatment education builds trust and sets the foundation for satisfaction. Practitioners should explain that the goal is softening and not the total erasure of lines. Discuss potential side effects like bruising, swelling, or rare vascular issues.

Contraindications include hypersensitivity to hyaluronic acid, autoimmune disorders, and active skin infections. Ensure patients sign informed consent and understand aftercare guidelines to minimize risk.

Post-Injection Aftercare Protocols

Proper aftercare helps optimize results and minimize potential complications following vertical lip line treatment with Juvéderm. Educating patients on essential steps ensures a smoother recovery and better outcomes:

Avoid pressure and lip movement: Do not press, pucker, or manipulate the lips for at least 24 hours.

Refrain from smoking and makeup: Smoking and applying makeup can increase the risk of irritation or infection and should be avoided during the first 24 hours.

Limit physical activity: Avoid strenuous exercise and alcohol consumption on the day of treatment.

Use cold compresses sparingly: If swelling occurs, apply a cold pack gently without pressing the area.

Sleep with head elevated: This can help reduce swelling overnight.

Monitor for complications: Watch for signs like blanching, prolonged pain, or discoloration, which may indicate vascular compromise and require prompt medical attention.

FAQs

What is the best lip filler for smokers lines?

Juvéderm Volbella XC is commonly regarded as the best filler for smoker’s lines due to its soft consistency and compatibility with superficial placement. It offers smooth integration for delicate perioral skin.

Where not to inject lip fillers?

Lip fillers should not be injected into major blood vessels, the wet-dry border (vermillion border) without precision, or too superficially, as this can lead to the Tyndall effect or nodules.

Is Botox or Juvéderm better for lip lines?

Botox and Juvéderm serve different purposes. Botox relaxes the muscle responsible for dynamic wrinkles, while Juvéderm fills static lines. They are often used together for optimal results.
