What is Alcohol Usage Disorder?

Alcohol use disorder is a condition with continued heavy and/or frequent alcohol use despite mental, social, or physical problems. It is sometimes referred to as alcoholism.

Typical Symptoms of Alcohol Usage Disorder

I no longer function well at work or home because of drinking

Need a drink first thing in the morning (eye-opener) to steady my nerves

Hand tremors, irritability, palpitations and insomnia that occur when stopping alcohol

I always end up drinking more than I planned to

Need to drink more than before, to get drunk

Diagnostic Questions for Alcohol Usage Disorder

Your doctor may ask these questions to check for this disease:

Are you drinking more and neglecting family or job?

Do you often drink alcohol in the morning to cure a hangover?

Do you experience hand shaking or irritation after quitting alcohol?

Do you often drink more alcohol than planned?

Do you need more alcohol to get drunk recently?

Treatment of Alcohol Usage Disorder

Treatment usually involves a combination of medication, support, and therapy.
