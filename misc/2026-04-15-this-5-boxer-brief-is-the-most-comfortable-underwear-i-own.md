What to Consider

Fabric

Cotton

Soft, breathable, and naturally moisture-absorbing. Cotton is a classic choice that feels comfy and is great for everyday wear. The downside? It can retain moisture, which may not be ideal for intense workouts or hot, humid weather.

Modal

A type of rayon made from beech trees, modal is super soft, breathable, and lightweight. It’s also moisture-wicking and holds up well over time, making it a great option for all-day wear.

Spandex/Elastane (Lycra)

[Most Comfortable Underwear Own](https://incretin-path.escortskhi.com/)

Known for its stretch, this fabric is often blended with cotton or other materials to give boxer briefs extra flexibility and shape retention. It offers a snug fit and allows for full range of motion, perfect for activewear.

Bamboo

Eco-friendly and incredibly soft, bamboo fabric is naturally moisture-wicking, odor-resistant, and breathable. It’s also hypoallergenic, making it a great choice for sensitive skin.

Tencel (Lyocell)

A sustainable fabric made from wood pulp, Tencel is soft, breathable, and moisture-wicking. It’s naturally cool to the touch and feels luxurious while offering good stretch and support.

Fit and Pouch

It's not just about the size. First, consider the pouch design. Does the brand make an effort to give you enough room for the boys to move around and breathe? If not, skip the try-on phase and go right to the next. And don't underestimate the benefits of finding the right leg and the right leg length, otherwise known as inseam. Though it all depends on your preference, you'll want legs or inseams that won't move around or bunch in the skin, and don't add extra bulk when you're wearing pants.

Short Boxer Briefs (3-inch inseam)

This length is best for guys who prefer minimal coverage or wear shorter shorts. These hit high on the thigh, making them feel lightweight and less restrictive. They're great for freedom of movement, but can sometimes ride up, especially if the leg openings aren't snug enough.

Mid-Length Boxer Briefs (5-6 inch inseam)

This is the Goldilocks length—not too short, not too long. It provides solid thigh coverage while staying put under most pants, whether slim or relaxed fit. The added length also helps prevent chafing. Best for everyday wear and versatility.

Long Boxer Briefs (8-9 inch inseam)

These extend further down the leg, offering maximum coverage and anti-chafe protection. They're ideal for workouts or guys with larger thighs since they help reduce friction. The only downside? They make peek out under shorter shorts.

What Are the Most Supportive Boxer Briefs?

Boxer briefs are supportive by design, but the most supportive pairs pay especially close attention to one thing: pouch design.

The pouch design on a good pair of boxer briefs should give you just enough room and support for your goods to move around. Pick a pouch design that's too roomy and you start losing support. Pick a pouch design that's too restrictive and, well, we all know that's a fit we definitely don't want.

And to touch on another quality we mentioned, your boxer brief inseam is important. In terms of how inseam relates to support, a short to medium-length inseam will give you the most support, as the less length you have on your legs the closer you get to briefs, which is the most supportive kind of underwear you can buy. A longer inseam (say anything 7" or more) can create less support, as you start bringing some of the fabric away from your skin.But with a longer boxer brief inseam, you reduce the risk of chaffing (shorter inseam fits closer to the skin), so that's the trade-off you're getting when going longer.
