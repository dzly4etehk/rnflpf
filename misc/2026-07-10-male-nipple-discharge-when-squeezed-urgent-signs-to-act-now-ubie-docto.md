Possible causes range from benign conditions—such as hormonal imbalances, certain medications, or gynecomastia—to more serious issues like infections, pituitary tumors, or breast cancer. Explanation

Nipple discharge in males can happen for several reasons. According to the provided references, one common cause is hormonal changes, which can lead to a condition called benign galactorrhea. This is when milk or a milk-like fluid comes from the nipple, and it is usually not serious.

However, nipple discharge can also be a sign of other issues. For example, infections or inflammation in the breast tissue can cause discharge. Sometimes, the discharge may even be bloody, which can be concerning. In rare cases, nipple discharge might be linked to tumors or other growths in the breast tissue.
