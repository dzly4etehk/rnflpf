What is Microneedling with Exosomes?

Microneedling is a treatment in which small needles make minute, controlled wounds within the skin. These wounds stimulate your skin to mend. As a result, your body produces more collagen and elastin—proteins that keep the skin firm, smooth, and youthful.

Exosomes are small bubbles secreted from cells. In aesthetics, exosomes are harvested from cultured stem cells. They are loaded with healing messages such as growth factors, peptides, mRNA, cytokines, and lipids. Those assist the skin to heal more quickly.

When you combine exosomes with microneedling, you are not just triggering healing. You are giving the skin the right tools to repair itself more efficiently. The exosomes are administered along with or after microneedling and thus penetrate deeply into the skin through microchannels. This enhances cellular communication, accelerates recovery, and enhances overall skin quality.

FAQS

Is exosome microneedling worth it?

Yes, exosome microneedling is worth it for individuals who are looking for noticeable improvement in the skin but have little downtime. The therapy combines collagen stimulation through microneedling and regenerative benefits through exosomes, which provide smoother, tighter, and healthier-looking skin.

Is microneedling with exosomes better than PRP?

Microneedling with exosomes ranks as more advanced than using PRP for most clients. While PRP utilizes your own platelets, exosomes provide a more concentrated source of growth factors, peptides, and signalling molecules that work to facilitate deeper, more rapid skin repair.

How often should I do microneedling with exosomes?

Microneedling with exosomes is typically repeated every 4 to 6 weeks. Most clients benefit from 3 to 6 sessions depending on their skin concerns. A series can be followed by maintenance treatments every 4 to 6 months.

What are microneedling exosomes?

[with Exosomes Key](https://defensecascade.china-akan.com/)

Microneedling exosomes is a procedure involving the topical application of exosomes during or after microneedling. The micro-channel pathways opened by microneedling let the exosomes penetrate deep within the skin, improving healing, collagen synthesis, and overall skin condition.

What are the disadvantages of exosomes?

The primary drawback to exosomes is the price, which is higher than for standard serums or PRP. Long-term clinical data is still limited, although current studies show promising results.

What are the side effects of exosome microneedling?

Side effects from exosome microneedling are typically mild and transient. Redness, modest swelling, and a sensation of warmth within the skin for 24–48 hours are common. Allergic reaction is negligible due to the fact that exosomes are non-immunogenic and cell-free.

How much does exosome microneedling cost?

Exosome microneedling costs between £300 to £800 per session in most UK clinics. Prices vary based on the clinic’s location, the quality of the exosome product used, and the area being treated.

What is the downtime for exosome microneedling?

The downtime for exosome microneedling is minimal. The majority of patients can only experience mild redness for 24 to 48 hours, followed by a rapid return to normal activities. Inflammation is minimized, and healing is accelerated by exosomes.
