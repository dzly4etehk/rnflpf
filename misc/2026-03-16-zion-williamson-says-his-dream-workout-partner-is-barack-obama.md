# Zion Williamson Says His Dream Workout Partner Is Barack Obama

NBA star Zion Williamson says his dream workout partner would be President Barack Obama.

Williamson, who plays for the New Orleans Pelicans, was only 8 years old when Obama won his first election.

The two met earlier this year at the NBA's All-Star Weekend in Chicago.

[Zion Williamson](https://copperglow.dns0663.com/)

At 6'8'' and 284 pounds, there's little debate: Zion Williamson is a physical specimen. The burgeoning New Orleans Pelicans superstar only just completed his first year in the NBA, but he might already be one the league's strongest players. There's no doubt that a workout with him would be a hot ticket. But who would he most want to work out with? Longtime NBA writer JA Adande got the answer in the latest Men's Health cover story: President Barack Obama.

[Says His](https://tendonforge.dns0663.com/blog/0091175027.html)

[Dream Workout Partner](https://neurocurrent.china-akan.com/blog/4144676066.html)

Williamson was only 8 years old when Obama won his first election in 2008, but the man who led the country between January 20, 2009 and January 20 2017 would still be the forward's top choice, according to Adande's story. The two met for the first time earlier this year at an NBA Cares community service event in Chicago, during All Star Weekend. At that point, Obama gave Williamson a detailed analysis of his game both for the Pelicans and in college, when he played for legendary coach Mike Krzyzewski and the Duke Blue Devils.

[His Dream](https://calmvector.dns0663.com/)

[Dream Workout](https://benpepsi.com/)

[Barack Obama](https://reboundworks.escortskhi.com/)

Ben Golliver, who covers the NBA for The Washington Post, caught the meeting on video.

Before Williamson was the #1 overall pick, Obama was also present at perhaps the biggest showcase of his collegiate career: a late-season February 2019 matchup between Williamson's Duke Blue Devils and their rival North Carolina Tar Heels in Durham, North Carolina. The game was expected super-hyped, and was expected to be yet another chance for Williamson to show off his athletic and versatile game; instead, he ended up leaving early with an injury after cutting hard and his shoe literally exploding off his foot.

[Workout Partner](https://pigmentlab.jcesqc8.com/blog/3623490741.html)

At that point, Williamson was already considered the consensus top prospect for the NBA draft, but fans everywhere were concerned that this injury would put a wrench into those plans. Among those fans who wished him well? None other than President Barack Obama.

[Says His Dream Workout](https://glycometrix.snaketongueofficial.com/blog/8808769852.html)

"Zion Williamson seems like an outstanding young man as well as an outstanding basketball player," he wrote on February 20, 2019. "Wishing him a speedy recovery."

[Williamson Says](https://repair-cascade.jcesqc8.com/blog/0686784547.html)

So now that Williamson is at the end of his rookie year, healthy, and once again dominating, maybe that joint-workout can finally happen. What do you say, Mr. President?
