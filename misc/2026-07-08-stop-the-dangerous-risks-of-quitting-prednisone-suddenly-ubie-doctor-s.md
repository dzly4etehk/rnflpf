Internal Medicine

Do not stop taking prednisone suddenly. Abruptly discontinuing prednisone can cause serious health problems, including adrenal insufficiency, fatigue, body aches, nausea, and low blood pressure. Prednisone should always be tapered gradually under a doctor's supervision to allow your adrenal glands time to resume normal cortisol production.

If you're experiencing side effects, withdrawal symptoms, or new health concerns while taking or tapering prednisone, it's important to understand what's happening in your body before making any changes. Explanation

Prednisone is a type of medicine called a steroid. It is important not to stop taking prednisone suddenly because it can lead to health problems. According to the research, stopping prednisone suddenly can cause the body to have trouble making its own steroids, which are important for your health.

Tapering Prednisone

Gradual Reduction: It is usually safer to reduce the dose slowly, a process known as tapering. This helps your body adjust and start making its own steroids again.
