Medications

According to the medical literature, certain medications can help manage sciatica pain. These can include pain relievers that help reduce inflammation and ease discomfort.

Physical Therapy

Physical therapy is another option that can help. It involves exercises that strengthen your back and improve flexibility, which can help relieve pressure on the sciatic nerve.

Needling Interventions

Needling interventions such as acupuncture can also provide relief. This involves using thin needles to target specific points on the body, which may help reduce pain and improve function.
