What are the best foods to consume when experiencing diarrhea?

Reviewed by Yoshinori Abe, MD

Internal Medicine

If you have diarrhea, eating gentle, binding foods can help firm up your stool and ease digestion. Bananas are especially helpful because they are easy on the stomach and rich in potassium, which replaces electrolytes lost through frequent bowel movements. Foods high in pectin—a soluble fiber found in bananas, applesauce, and cooked apples—can also absorb excess water in the intestines, helping produce more solid stools. Other bland options like white rice, toast, and boiled potatoes are commonly recommended as part of the BRAT diet.

When you have diarrhea, it is important to eat foods that are gentle on your stomach and can help manage your symptoms. Green bananas are a good choice because they can help firm up your stool. They contain a type of fiber that is easy to digest and can make your stool less watery.

[Best Foods](https://pigmentlab.jcesqc8.com/)

Pectin, a type of fiber found in fruits like apples, can also be helpful. It helps to absorb water in your intestines, which can make your stool firmer. Eating foods with pectin can be a gentle way to help your stomach feel better.

It's important to keep eating small amounts of food even if you don't feel very hungry, as this can help your body recover and provides nutrition.
