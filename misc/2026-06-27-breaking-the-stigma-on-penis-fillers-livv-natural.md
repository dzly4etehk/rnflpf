Risks and considerations

Penis fillers are considered safe. However, as with any medical procedure, there are some potential risks. Among these are penile girth injection complications.

Below are some risks to keep in mind.

Infection: Inserting foreign substances into the body can trigger an immune response. These issues are more prevalent with synthetic options. The main way of treating the infection is by removing the filler.

Deformed penis and scarring: While the goal of fillers is to improve the appearance of the penis, sometimes, the opposite happens. In the worst cases, painful nodules develop. They can be treated with intense surgery, but tissue may be scarred. It could also result in reduced penis length.

Exposure to harmful products: This risk is associated with synthetic fillers. Certain ones are plastic. There is also some evidence that indicates the products are carcinogens and could cause cancer.

High cost on permanent options: This risk is also associated with synthetic options. They’re more expensive than bio-compatible ones. Despite this, many are of low quality and diluted.

Bruising and swelling: The penis is typically tender for two weeks after the procedure. It’s also common for the area to be swollen. In some cases, swelling can last for a month.

Many of these risks are easy to avoid. The most important precautionary measure is selecting a qualified and experienced practitioner. Make sure you feel comfortable discussing your options and requirements with the professional before committing to a treatment.

It’s also essential that you understand the extent of the treatment. Ask about the limitations of the procedure. Your practitioner should clarify the expected results before you agree to the filler.
