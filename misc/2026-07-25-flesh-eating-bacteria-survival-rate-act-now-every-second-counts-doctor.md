Necrotizing fasciitis, commonly known as flesh-eating bacteria infection, is a rare but life-threatening condition. Survival rates vary widely depending on how quickly it is diagnosed and treated, but overall mortality ranges from about 20% to 30%, and can climb higher when treatment is delayed or when the infection spreads to the bloodstream (sepsis).
