This is the reason it’s always important to shop smartly. No matter what’s being heralded as being in or out, there are certain styles that will never fall out of favor. Which is why we call them “classics,” after all.

Every wardrobe needs these pieces, which serve as the very foundation of personal style. From clean-cut trousers and jackets to unfussy base layers, they serve as building blocks that can be constructed in myriad ways. They can be dressed up or down. They surpass seasons. They remain constant go-tos, the stuff we reach for time and time again. Fully understanding all the above sentiments is Dockers. Historically known as a go-to for business casual ‘fits, the brand has evolved into a reliable one-stop shop for everyday casualwear. Chinos, tees, shorts, sportcoats, sweaters, and more: Dockers prioritizes quality and cut, consistently offering styles that not only work for a variety of scenarios, but also stand the test of time. Indeed, while trends come and go as they always do, the collection from Dockers is as close to everlasting as it gets.

If you’re in need of pieces to fill in the gaps—or even a whole style overhaul—make sure to check out the selection below and the different ways you can style each option.

Checks and Balances

The chino is an integral part of a man’s wardrobe: a trouser usually made of pure cotton, sometimes mixed with synthetic fibers for extra stretch, that comes in just about every hue, khaki being the go-to. We all need to cop chinos, especially this pair. The same can be said of a blazer, preferably in a cream color, that matches with everything. For some texture, and yet another level of dynamism, add a check shirt (or any geometric pattern) and brown leather shoes.

True Blue

This color comes in many shades, but the two absolute essentials are navy and chambray. The latter is cast on this shacket, which meanders seamlessly from ease to elegance and back again. The same goes for these trousers—chinos, cinched with a braided belt, that will take you from boardroom to bar. Not to be outdone is the stalwart white tee, the ultimate base layer.

Chill Out

On sunny days when the air feels crisp, select this winning combination: a pair of shorts that's as durable as it is breezy; a sleek button-down; a graphic crewneck sweater for when a cool breeze passes by; and streamlined white sneakers. All these ingredients together form a 'fit that's, simply put, chef's kiss.
