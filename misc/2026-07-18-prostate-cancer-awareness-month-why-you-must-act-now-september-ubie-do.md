When is prostate cancer awareness month observed?

Reviewed by Yoshinori Abe, MD

Internal Medicine

Prostate Cancer Awareness Month is observed every September in the United States. This month-long campaign raises awareness about prostate cancer, the second most common cancer among men, and encourages early screening, education, and support for those affected.

Because early-stage prostate cancer often causes no symptoms, and later signs (like urinary changes, pelvic discomfort, or unexplained fatigue) can overlap with many other conditions, it's easy to overlook warning signs. Understanding your risk early can make a meaningful difference in outcomes. If you've noticed anything unusual, take a free, instant, online symptom check to better understand your symptoms and confidently plan your next steps.
