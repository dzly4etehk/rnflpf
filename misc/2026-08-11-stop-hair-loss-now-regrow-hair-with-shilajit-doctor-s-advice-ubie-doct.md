What Is Shilajit?

Shilajit is a sticky substance found mainly in the Himalayas and parts of Central Asia. It forms over centuries from the slow decomposition of plants and microorganisms. Key components include:

Fulvic acid – a natural antioxidant that may protect cells from damage

Humic acids – support nutrient absorption

Minerals – iron, zinc, magnesium, selenium, and more

Traditional use spans over 3,000 years in Ayurveda, where it's prized for boosting energy, immunity, and overall wellbeing.

How Shilajit May Help Hair Density

While research on shilajit and hair growth is still emerging, several mechanisms could explain its potential:

Antioxidant Protection

Fulvic acid scavenges free radicals that can damage hair follicles.

Reducing oxidative stress may promote a healthier scalp environment.

Mineral Support

Zinc and iron are crucial for hair protein synthesis (keratin).

Deficiencies in these minerals are linked to thinning hair.

Hormonal Balance

Some studies suggest shilajit can support healthy testosterone levels in men.

Balanced hormones contribute to normal hair growth cycles.

What the Research Says

A small clinical trial showed shilajit supplementation improved markers of oxidative stress and testosterone levels in men (source: Journal of Ethnopharmacology).

More large-scale, placebo-controlled trials are needed to confirm benefits for hair density in males.
