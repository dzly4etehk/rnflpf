Frequent urination in men is often associated with lower urinary tract symptoms (LUTS), which become more common as men age.

Key Points:

Benign Prostatic Hyperplasia (BPH): This condition, where the prostate gland enlarges, is a primary cause of frequent urination in older men. According to the research, BPH can lead to urinary symptoms such as nocturia, which is waking up at night to urinate.

Prevalence: Studies indicate that lower urinary tract symptoms, including frequent urination, are prevalent among men aged 45 and older. This prevalence increases with age.

Nocturia: This specific symptom of waking up to urinate at night is particularly noted in men with BPH, highlighting the impact of prostate health on urinary habits.

Understanding these symptoms and their causes can help in managing and seeking appropriate treatment.
