Secondary infertility with PCOS refers to difficulty conceiving again after a previous pregnancy, typically caused by irregular ovulation, hormonal imbalances, or insulin resistance associated with polycystic ovary syndrome. Even after a successful first pregnancy, PCOS symptoms can worsen or shift over time, making conception harder.

Explanation

Secondary infertility means having trouble getting pregnant again after having at least one child. For people with PCOS, this can happen because of problems like irregular periods or issues with hormones.
