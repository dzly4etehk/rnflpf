# What to Do When Your Tattoo Goes Wrong

You're inked up, and you're doing everything your tattoo artist and dermatologist recommend. You've got the proper lotion, keep the area clean, and abide by all the skincare tips to assure your tattoo's long-term care. But something's not right.

Maybe the skin is reddening, or the tattoo itself already looks like you've had it for decades — it's faded or losing color. Although it's not ideal, you're not alone. Whether it's your first tattoo or your fifth, things can go wrong. For anyone dealing with problems with their ink, we've got solutions.

Even if you're doing aftercare right, you could still end up with an infection, color bleeding, or fading. Joshua Zeichner, M.D., an associate professor of dermatology at Mount Sinai Hospital in New York, troubleshoots three common issues, and provides advice for what you should do.
