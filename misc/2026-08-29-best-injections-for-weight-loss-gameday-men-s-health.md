What Are Weight Loss Injections?

Weight loss injections include prescription medications and nutrient blends delivered into fatty tissue or muscle. Unlike over-the-counter diet pills or “fat-burning” teas, these treatments are prescribed by a healthcare professional and monitored for safety and results.

These injections typically work in one or more of the following ways: (2)

Regulating appetite by influencing hunger hormones and slowing stomach emptying

Improving blood sugar control and reducing insulin resistance, which reduces cravings

2. Tirzepatide (Mounjaro®, Zepbound™)

Tirzepatide is a dual-acting medication that targets both GLP-1 and GIP receptors—two key hormone pathways that control appetite and metabolism. Some studies have shown that tirzepatide can potentially be even more effective at treating weight loss than semaglutide, with one indicating that tirzpepatide patients lost over 6% more body fat than semaglutide patients. (7)Tirzepatide may be a strong option for men with a significant amount of weight to lose or those who haven’t responded as well to other treatments. Like semaglutide, it’s typically administered once weekly and requires gradual dose escalation to reduce side effects, which commonly include diarrhea, constipation, nausea, or upset stomach. (8)

3. Liraglutide (Saxenda®)

Liraglutide is another GLP-1 agonist, but unlike semaglutide and tirzepatide, it requires daily injections.

Liraglutide has been on the market longer than its newer counterparts, giving it a well-documented results and safety record. For example, one study showed that liraglutide use resulted in an average of 4 to 6 kg (8.8 to 13.2 lbs.) with the most common side effects being gastrointestinal issues. (9)

Your provider may recommend liraglutide if you can’t tolerate other GLP-1 medications or if it’s a better fit for your treatment plan based on health history and goals.

4. B12 & Lipotropic Injections

Lipotropic injections combine B12 with amino acids and nutrients such as methionine, inositol, and choline.These compounds may help support liver function, fat metabolism, and energy production. (10)

While they’re typically not as powerful for weight loss as GLP-1 medications, they can be a useful complement to diet and exercise—especially for men dealing with low energy or sluggish metabolism, though clinical studies supporting significant weight loss are limited.

Lipotropic injections can be administered weekly or bi-weekly, depending on your program. They’re often part of a broader medical weight loss strategy rather than a standalone treatment.
