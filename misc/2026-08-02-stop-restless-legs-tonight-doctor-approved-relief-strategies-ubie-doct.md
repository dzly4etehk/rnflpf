Explanation

Restless Legs Syndrome (RLS) can make it hard to sleep due to an uncomfortable feeling in the legs. Here are some strategies that might help:

Stretching and Massage: Gently stretching or massaging your legs before bed can help ease the discomfort.

Heat or Cold Packs: Applying heat or cold packs to your legs might provide relief. You can use a warm towel or a cold pack.

Lifestyle Changes:

[Tonight Doctor-Approved](https://beautybiometrics.hbsnzj.com/)

Reduce Caffeine and Alcohol: Cutting back on caffeine and alcohol might help lessen RLS symptoms.

Exercise: Regular, moderate exercise can be beneficial, but avoid intense workouts close to bedtime.

Good Sleep Habits: Keep a regular sleep schedule and create a comfortable sleeping environment.

If these strategies don't help, it's important to talk to a healthcare provider.
