What methods can stop diarrhea without using medications?

Reviewed by Yoshinori Abe, MD

Internal Medicine

To stop diarrhea without medicine, try eating bland, easily digestible foods like bananas, rice, applesauce, and toast (the BRAT diet). Staying hydrated with water, clear broths, or oral rehydration solutions is also essential, as diarrhea can quickly lead to fluid loss. Resting your digestive system by avoiding dairy, caffeine, fatty foods, and sugary drinks can further support recovery.

However, diarrhea can sometimes signal a more serious underlying condition, especially if it lasts more than a couple of days or is accompanied by fever, blood, or severe pain.
