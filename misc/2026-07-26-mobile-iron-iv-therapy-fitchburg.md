What can Iron IV therapy help with?

Our iron IV therapy is typically used to address symptoms related to anemia or iron deficiency. If your body isn’t producing enough iron on its own, it can’t produce hemoglobin, which the body uses to transport oxygen in your blood. By delivering iron directly into your bloodstream, we can replenish your iron levels more effectively than oral supplements. Our therapy can relieve symptoms, such as: Fatigue or low energy Brittle nails Dizziness or lightheadedness Restless legs syndrome Headaches Dull-looking skin

Does Iron IV therapy have side effects?

Like with any treatment, iron IV therapy may have mild and temporary side effects. You may experience mild irritation at the injection site, slight nausea, temporary dizziness, and a mild headache. These side effects are temporary and often resolve on their own. A licensed provider will be with you throughout your treatment to ensure your safety.

How often should I do Iron IV therapy?

How often you should receive iron IV therapy treatments will depend on the severity of your iron deficiency, your lab results, and your health goals. For some clients, one infusion is enough. Others may need multiple sessions to restore their iron levels. The best approach is to base your treatment on lab results as well as medical guidance.

Is Iron IV safe in pregnancy?

Iron IV therapy can be prescribed during pregnancy when you have an iron deficiency, and treatment is necessary. However, the infusion must be done under a doctor’s supervision, and you must be closely monitored. If you are pregnant, consult with our Drip Hydration team as well as your OB-GYN.

How long does Iron IV therapy last?

Iron IV therapy can last for several months, depending on your baseline iron levels and your overall health.
