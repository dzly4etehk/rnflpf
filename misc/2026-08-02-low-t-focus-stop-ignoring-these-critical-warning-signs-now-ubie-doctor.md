Explanation

Testosterone is not only important for building muscles and energy but also for helping the brain concentrate and think clearly. When testosterone levels are low, some men notice that they have trouble keeping their focus, remembering things, or processing information as quickly as before. This is because testosterone acts like fuel for brain function, supporting memory and attention.

Lower levels of testosterone can mean that the brain gets less of this fuel, which may slow down mental processes and affect clarity of thought. Research has shown that men with low testosterone sometimes experience more difficulty with concentration and may feel foggier or less alert than usual.
