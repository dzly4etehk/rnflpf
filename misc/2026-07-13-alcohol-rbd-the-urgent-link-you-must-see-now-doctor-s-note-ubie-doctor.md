[Now Doctor Note](https://regeneration-desk.hbsnzj.com/blog/3955266262.html)

REM Sleep Behavior Disorder (RBD) is a parasomnia in which people physically act out their dreams during REM sleep. Research links RBD to alcohol misuse, sedative-hypnotic withdrawal, and certain substance use, which can disrupt normal REM sleep regulation and trigger episodes. Beyond substances, RBD may also signal underlying neurological conditions, making early evaluation important.
