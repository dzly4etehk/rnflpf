# Supersets Workout to Build Muscle and Burn Fat at the Same Time

I use supersets in almost all of my total body workouts from www.TurbulenceTraining.com, using 2-3 supersets of multi-muscle exercises to get the most work done in the least amount of time, while also building strength (and muscle if that is the goal).

So a typical total-body, multi-muscle workout would look like this:

1A) Squat type movement 1B) Pushing exercise

[Supersets Workout](https://hostdefense-journal.escortskhi.com/)

2A) Back of the legs exercise 2B) Pulling exercise

3A) Single leg exercise 3B) Total body ab exercise

Supersets simply save time while allowing a high intensity of training to be maintained. While legs rest,chest works, and vice versa (in Superset #1 above).

[the Same Time](https://innate-signal.jcesqc8.com/blog/9278616845.html)

I use non-competing exercises in my supersets - meaning two exercises that don't interfere with one another. The one thing you have to watch out for that most trainers miss is to avoid pairing exercises that compete for grip.

[at the Same](https://pigmentlab.jcesqc8.com/)

[Supersets Workout to](https://tissue-renewal.china-akan.com/)

[Muscle and](https://allpepay.com/)

For example, dumbbell lunges and dumbbell rows are a bad pair of exercises...too much grip work - therefore, they are "competing" exercises.

Guys always ask, how much rest should I take in a superset?

Well, in a fat burning workout we dont rest within the superset, but rest 60 seconds before repeating. Nothing is set in stone though. It depends on the exercises, goal, etc. I like at least 60 seconds because most people don't sacrifice too much strength.

Sometimes we take more rest in the first superset if we are working with a woman on chinups or other "max strength" type bodyweight exercises.

With guys, when building muscle and more absolute strength, we'll take longer within and between superset rests.

Another common question is, "If supersets are good, why not do trisets or circuits? Aren't they better?"

And I always say, yes, they are good, and you might even be able to shorten the workout even more in some cases. However, one big negative is that now you're getting into logistics problems. Do you have the equipment/space to set up a triset? Plus, you have to worry more about non-competing exercise issues.

Supersets are a great way to build muscle and burn fat at the same time,
