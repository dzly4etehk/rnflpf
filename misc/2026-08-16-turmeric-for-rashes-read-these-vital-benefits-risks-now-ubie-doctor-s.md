Turmeric, a spice often used in cooking, has properties that might help treat skin issues. One of its components, Ar-Turmerone, found in turmeric oil, has been shown to have antidermatophytic properties. This means it might help fight off certain fungi that can cause skin problems.

However, turmeric is not without risks. Some people might be allergic to curcumin, a component of turmeric, which can actually cause skin rashes instead of helping them.
