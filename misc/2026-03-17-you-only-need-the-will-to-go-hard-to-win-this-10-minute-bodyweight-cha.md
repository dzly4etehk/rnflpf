# You Only Need the Will to Go Hard to Win This 10-Minute Bodyweight Challenge

While the coronavirus (COVID-19) pandemic has halted the cadence of everyday life for just about everyone, the world is now beginning to adjust. We're all handling the challenges of social distancing and self-isolation differently—but that doesn't mean that we have to go it completely alone in every sense.

At Men's Health, we're using this period as an opportunity to build up our community and share as much useful, positive information as possible. For everyone stuck missing their typical workout routines with gyms and fitness centers closed down, we've done our best to provide as many at-home workout options as possible. But that's just the start. We're also hosting live workout sessions on Instagram with some of our favorite trainers to fill the fitness class-shaped void in your daily routine.

Bobby Maximus, former UFC fighter, fitness personality, and author of the Men's Health Maximus Body book, hosted the latest session. Maximus makes a point to train without equipment even when he has a fully-stocked gym at his disposal—so creating an accessible session for guys stuck at home without access to gear fits perfectly to his training style.

You're actually going to need one thing for today's session, according to Maximus: the will to go hard. (Having a timer will also help.) He often finds that people are more likely to give excuses to why they can't exercise than taking the opportunity to work hard and push through inconvenience—so don't let that be you. After all, the workout is only 10 minutes long.

The reason why the workout will still be challenging is the format. You'll use the Every Minute on the Minute (EMOM) protocol, which requires you to perform a predetermined number of reps for every minute in a set period of time. Usually, the setup is designed to allow you to rest for the remainder of each minute until the next begins—but Maximus has other ideas. To pump as much volume as possible into this workout, your rest time will become work time with a second exercise.
